🏆 What happened this week

Michigan won… but did they?

This might be my favorite story from the weekend.

No. 16 Michigan was expected to handle Western Michigan pretty easily. Instead? Western Michigan nearly pulled off a massive upset.

Michigan threw a Hail Mary as time expired, it didn’t work, and Western Michigan’s sideline rushed the field thinking they had won.

Except officials reviewed the play and decided there was actually ONE SECOND left on the clock.

Michigan got one more chance. They threw another Hail Mary. Touchdown. Michigan wins 13–12.

Yes. Seriously.

Naturally, people are now arguing about whether that final second should have existed at all. Western Michigan went from celebrating one of the biggest wins in program history to losing approximately 30 seconds later.

College football is cruel.

🐯 College football

LSU absolutely blew out Clemson.

Remember last week when I told you LSU vs. Clemson was the college football game to know?

Well… LSU won 51–10.

That is not “we had a good game.” That is “hello, please include us in the national championship conversation.”

It was also Lane Kiffin’s first game as LSU’s head coach, so casually remember that name. I have a feeling we’re going to be talking about him plenty this season.

🦆 College football

Oregon survived.

No. 2 Oregon was supposed to be one of the best teams in the country. Boise State apparently did not care.

Oregon trailed at halftime before eventually escaping with a 34–27 win. They’re still undefeated, but it was definitely closer than Oregon fans would’ve preferred.

And that’s the fun part about early college football: we have absolutely no idea who is actually good yet.

☕️ What’s the tea?

Sir, you DO NOT play for the 49ers.

I genuinely cannot believe this is a real story.

A man named Daejon Love allegedly spent years convincing people that he played wide receiver for the San Francisco 49ers.

The problem? He did not play for the San Francisco 49ers.

Federal prosecutors accuse Love and an accomplice of using the fake NFL-player persona as part of a scheme that allegedly took more than $1.3 million from 26 women.

And this man apparently COMMITTED to the bit. Replica helmet. 49ers gear. Videos. The whole production.

Actual 49ers players have now been asked about him, and even they can’t believe how ridiculous the story is.

Ladies, I apparently have to add this to the dating advice portion of Fi’s Playbook: If he says he plays in the NFL, perhaps check the roster.
