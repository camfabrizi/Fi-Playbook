🚨 First things first

Carolina keeps surviving, Kansas City has a new champion & Thanksgiving is serving football ALL day.

For the people who don’t watch sports… but still want to know what’s going on.

A quick rundown of the biggest stories in sports—without assuming you know every player, rule, or stat. Whether you’re at happy hour, in the office, watching with friends, or on a date with a sports fanatic, you’ll have something to contribute to the conversation.

I don’t know who scheduled Thanksgiving week, but I would personally like to thank them. The Panthers won AGAIN. Kansas City Current finally got its championship. The 49ers absolutely embarrassed Minnesota in Mexico City and climbed back to the top of the power rankings. The Giants upset Jacksonville. College football once again destroyed everybody’s playoff predictions. And now we get three NFL games on Thanksgiving, including Mahomes vs. Allen in primetime. Please clear your plate and your schedule. Let’s get into it.

🏆 What happened this week

🐾 The Panthers are doing this to me again.

Carolina beat Baltimore 35–33 in yet another game that probably took several years off my life. The Panthers went down 12–0, trailed 26–22 at halftime, came back, lost the lead AGAIN in the fourth quarter, and then Bryce Young casually led another game-winning drive to set up Ryan Fitzgerald’s 37-yard field goal with 1:44 left. The defense sealed it with an interception.

Bryce finished with 254 yards and two touchdowns, while Chuba Hubbard ran for 120 yards and two scores. Carolina is now 5–5, has won two straight and sits two games up in the NFC South. Are we dominant? No. Are we somehow winning? YES. I’ll take it.

🏆 NWSL

KC Current finally got their trophy.

The Kansas City Current are NWSL champions for the first time. KC beat the Washington Spirit 2–1 in the final, with Vanessa DiBernardo scoring directly from a corner kick — yes, an Olimpico — before Debinha added another goal deep into stoppage time. DiBernardo was named Championship MVP. Washington scored in the final moments, but it was too late.

And after the Spirit reached the championship for the third straight year? They finished runner-up for the third straight year. Ouch. Kansas City, meanwhile, finally gets to say: Champions.

🔥 NFL

San Fran just made a statement.

Remember when Minnesota had one of the scariest defenses in football? San Francisco apparently did not care. The 49ers absolutely handled the Vikings 35–10 in Mexico City, improving to 9–1. Brock Purdy threw three touchdowns, George Kittle caught two of them, and Mike Evans returned from injury with 109 receiving yards and a score.

And after that performance, San Francisco has climbed back to No. 1 in multiple Week 12 power rankings. Honestly? Hard to argue with it. The 49ers are 9–1 and just made another NFC contender look VERY average.

🗽 NFL

The Giants just knocked off Jacksonville.

This one caught me off guard. The Giants beat the Jaguars 23–18, handing Jacksonville only its third loss of the season. New York jumped out to a 20–6 halftime lead and held on despite Jacksonville making things interesting late. The Giants are now 6–3–1 and somehow sitting in first place in the NFC East.

Meanwhile, Jacksonville went from looking like one of the hottest teams in football to getting humbled by New York. The NFL remains committed to making zero sense.

🏈 College football

College football blew up again.

Just when we thought the rankings might calm down… Absolutely not. Unranked Michigan beat No. 4 UCLA 18–16 in TRIPLE overtime. Washington beat No. 8 Indiana 27–24 in overtime. Nebraska beat Ohio State 31–28, basically ending the Buckeyes’ playoff hopes. Oh — and Rutgers beat Penn State 25–24 as a 24.5-point underdog.

At this point, Rivalry Week arriving this weekend feels dangerous.

☕️ What’s the tea?

The booth is beefing…

Okayyyyy this one has layers. Joe Buck and Troy Aikman have been broadcasting together for 25 years. They left Fox together. They moved to ESPN together. They are literally one of those duos you just assume comes as a package. Apparently… not anymore.

Buck reportedly signed a new six-year extension with ESPN worth north of $100 million. Aikman? His contract expires after this season, and ESPN reportedly hasn’t even given him a formal offer yet. And THAT apparently did not go over well.

Reports say the two actually got into an argument in the booth before a Monday Night Football game earlier this season. They’ve still been completely professional on TV, but behind the scenes? Things apparently aren’t exactly warm and fuzzy.

And then Troy decided to make it JUICIER. He was on a Dallas radio show last week when one of the hosts joked about doing the show alone. Troy’s response? “Joe’s always wanted a one-man booth.” SIR??? 😭

And because apparently ESPN would like everyone to STOP TALKING ABOUT THIS, chairman Jimmy Pitaro publicly called the media coverage surrounding their feud “disappointing.”

Aikman is expected to become a free agent after ESPN broadcasts the Super Bowl in February, and reports increasingly suggest this could be the end of the longest-running national NFL broadcast duo. So basically… They still have to sit next to each other every Monday night, call the Super Bowl together, smile for the cameras… and then potentially break up.

Workplace tension but make it nationally televised. I will absolutely be analyzing the body language now. ☕️

🛋️ Fi’s couch report

Okay QB1.

Okay. I think I have officially accepted that the Panthers are simply going to make every win as stressful as humanly possible. We were down 12–0. Then we came back. Then Baltimore went back ahead. Then Bryce led another fourth-quarter drive. Then Ryan Fitzgerald kicked ANOTHER late field goal. And then the defense intercepted Baltimore to finally end it. My heart rate? Unwell.

But I have to give Bryce his flowers. He threw two touchdowns, completed over 73% of his passes, and once again looked completely calm when the game actually mattered—and seeing him stand up for himself when things got heated with Trey Hendrickson? Okay QB1. I liked that.

Chuba also continues to be the grown man in the room. 120 rushing yards and two touchdowns. Just give him the ball and let him work.

Is this team perfect? Absolutely not. But we’ve now won two straight close games, we’re 5–5, and somehow we have a two-game lead in the NFC South. And after spending the last few seasons begging for competitive football? I am TRYING to appreciate this without getting delusional.

Unfortunately, Tampa on Monday night feels exactly like the type of game that could make me delusional. So… KEEP POUNDING 💙
