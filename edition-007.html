🏀 What happened this week

The Kawhi Leonard situation is… a lot.

The NBA found that the Clippers and Kawhi Leonard violated salary-cap circumvention rules through outside business opportunities and endorsement arrangements.

The punishment was massive: the Clippers were fined $30 million and lost five future first-round draft picks. Owner Steve Ballmer was suspended from team and league activities for one year, and Leonard was ordered to pay $700,000.

Why it matters: first-round picks are a major way teams add elite young talent. Losing one in every draft from 2029 through 2033 could affect the Clippers long after the fine is paid.
