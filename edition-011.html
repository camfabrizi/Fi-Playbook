🎰 Sports Betting 101

What the heck does “-3.5” mean?

Okay, I got a request for this one and I realized something: Sports betting terminology sounds WAY more complicated than it actually is.

And before we begin: Fi’s Playbook is not telling you to gamble. I’m just making sure that if someone starts talking about their parlay at the bar, you know what the hell they’re talking about.

Consider this education, not financial advice. Now, let’s keep it simple.

➖ The spread
You’ll see something like: Bills -3.5. That means Buffalo is favored to win, and for a bet on Buffalo to win, they need to win by more than 3.5 points. If you see: Panthers +3.5, Carolina can either win the game OR lose by three points or fewer and that bet still wins. The half-point exists specifically so we don’t end in a tie.

💰 Moneyline
This is the easiest one. You’re simply picking who wins the game. No worrying about how many points. Just: who wins? The favorite will usually have a minus (-) number and the underdog a plus (+) number.

🔢 Over/under
You’re not picking a winner at all. You’re betting whether the combined score will finish above or below a number set by the sportsbook. If the over/under is 47.5: Final score 28–24 = 52 total points → OVER. Final score 20–17 = 37 → UNDER.

🧩 Parlay
This is where people get themselves into trouble. A parlay combines multiple bets into one bet. Every pick has to hit for the parlay to win. More picks = potentially bigger payout. Also: more ways for one random player to ruin your entire evening.

Translation: When someone says, “That touchdown just killed my parlay,” you now know why they’re suddenly staring into the distance. And remember: sports betting should be entertainment, not a way to make money. Set a budget you’re comfortable losing and do not chase a loss.

But now, if your boyfriend says, “Babe, let me sign you up for DraftKings so I can get the bonus bet,” you can at least respond: “Fine. But explain the rollover requirements.”

Just kidding. But hopefully now you can actually use that bonus bet without blindly clicking buttons and hoping for the best.

🤯 Sports fun fact

Ever wonder why football uniforms are SO tight? It’s not just because apparently every NFL player needs to show us exactly how many squats they do. 😭

The tighter fit actually serves a purpose: less loose fabric means less for an opposing player to grab onto during a tackle. Jerseys are even fitted around the shoulder pads and torso to keep extra material to a minimum.

So yes, the tiny pants are functional. But they are still very much tiny pants. 😂

💬 One thing to bring up

“Wait… what was the spread?”

That’s it. That’s your line. Now that you know what the spread actually means, this is an elite way to enter a sports conversation without needing to know every single thing about the game.

If someone says a team won by 3, you can casually ask, “Wait, what was the spread?” Because a team can win the actual game… and still not cover the spread. Look at you. Talking sports betting.
