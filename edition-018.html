🚨 First things first

Flights stopped, flags flew & somehow the Panthers are in first place.

For the people who don’t watch sports… but still want to know what’s going on.

A quick rundown of the biggest stories in sports—without assuming you know every player, rule, or stat. Whether you’re at happy hour, in the office, watching with friends, or on a date with a sports fanatic, you’ll have something to contribute to the conversation.

I genuinely don’t even know where to start this week. The FAA grounded flights across the COUNTRY and threw the entire sports schedule into chaos. Ole Miss had to move its game to Sunday. The Panthers committed approximately 700 penalties and somehow WON. The Chargers finally won a football game. Spain’s ridiculous unbeaten streak ended.

And apparently ABC looked at its list of eligible bachelors and said… give us the football player whose ex compared him to asparagus.

A lot to unpack. Let’s get into it.

🏆 What happened this week

🐾 We won. Please don’t ask how.

The Carolina Panthers are officially 4–5 and FIRST in the NFC South.

I know. I also have questions.

Carolina beat New Orleans 20–17 on a 54-yard Ryan Fitzgerald field goal as time expired, ending a three-game losing streak. Chuba Hubbard basically carried the offense on his back with 172 yards from scrimmage and two touchdowns.

Cute!

Now let’s discuss the other number. 17 penalties. For 152 yards. Both franchise records.

New Orleans? Three penalties.

And somehow… WE WON.

Was it pretty? Absolutely not. Does first place care about style points? Also no.

✈️ Travel chaos

The FAA grounded… sports?

Friday afternoon, the FAA issued a nationwide ground stop after communications issues at regional air-traffic-control centers. For several hours, basically nobody was flying.

Which became a HUGE problem when half the sports world was trying to travel for weekend games. College football teams were stranded at airports. Some teams gave up and took buses. NBA and NHL teams scrambled for hotels. Officials couldn’t reach their assigned NFL games.

The ground stop lasted more than four hours and forced leagues across the country to start improvising.

And that brings us to…

🏈 College football

Ole Miss got a Sunday game… and Oklahoma was not happy.

Ole Miss couldn’t get to Norman Friday night because of the FAA mess, so the SEC moved its Saturday game against Oklahoma to Sunday.

Oklahoma? Did NOT appreciate that.

Their athletic director publicly objected and said the SEC basically told Oklahoma: play Sunday… or forfeit.

So Oklahoma showed up Sunday. And beat Ole Miss 24–17.

Imagine rearranging your entire weekend, publicly arguing with your conference and then winning anyway.

Honestly? Respect.

⚡️ NFL

The Chargers finally won a football game.

THE STREAK IS OVER.

Los Angeles beat Baltimore 23–20 Monday night for its first win of the season, improving to a deeply inspiring 1–8.

And somehow the Chargers completely bullied Baltimore on the ground. LA outrushed the Ravens 160–16.

Derrick Henry? 10 carries. 10 yards.

The Chargers had lost 11 straight games dating back to last season.

So congratulations! It only took until mid-November.

⚽️ Nations League

Spain finally lost.

England beat Spain 3–2 in Madrid, ending Spain’s ridiculous 43-match unbeaten run.

FORTY-THREE.

For context, Spain hadn’t lost since before some of us developed several entirely new personality traits.

Meanwhile, Germany apparently chose violence and beat the Netherlands 5–0 the next day.

International break said drama only.

🚨 Sports chaos headlines

Sports remain deeply unserious.

🐔 SOUTH CAROLINA GOT HUMBLED. After winning four straight, the Gamecocks went to Arkansas and lost 34–6. Siri… please stop Sandstorm.

🤘🏼 TEXAS WON WITH 3 SECONDS LEFT. Texas beat LSU 22–21 after Arch Manning threw the game-winning touchdown with three seconds remaining. That is just rude.

🇩🇪 GERMANY SAID 5 IS ENOUGH. Germany demolished the Netherlands 5–0 to win its Nations League group. Four of those goals came in an 18-minute stretch.

👶🏻 JEREMIAH FEARS MADE HISTORY. The 20-year-old recorded his first career triple-double and became the youngest Pelican ever to do it.

⚽️ THE NWSL FINAL IS SET. Washington Spirit vs. Kansas City Current. Washington is going to its third straight championship, while KC knocked off No. 1 seed Gotham to get there.

☕️ What’s the tea?

The Bachelor has entered the huddle.

ABC announced its newest Bachelor. And naturally… it’s a quarterback.

Former Wake Forest and Notre Dame QB Sam Hartman will be the lead for Season 30 of The Bachelor. Hartman is currently on Washington’s practice squad and has yet to play a regular-season NFL snap.

But apparently? He’s ready for fantasy suites.

And THEN one of his exes was asked what dating him was like. Her description?

“The personality of asparagus.” 😭

She basically said Sam is a football, fishing and family guy whose idea of switching things up is… trying a different fishing spot. She DID also say he’s down-to-earth, family-oriented and “husband material.”

But I fear once you publicly compare a man’s personality to a vegetable… that is the headline.

So this spring we get: 30 women. One quarterback. Approximately 400 references to “finding his teammate for life.”

I can already hear the producers typing. ☕️

🛋️ Fi’s couch report

A win is a win.

Let me start with this: a win is a win.

We are first in the NFC South. I will take it.

NOW.

Can we please discuss the fact that we had SEVENTEEN PENALTIES??? Because apparently Friday’s FAA ground stop didn’t just mess with the teams. It messed with the refs too.

The NFL had to completely reshuffle officiating assignments, and Panthers–Saints ended up with a crew led by backup referee Duane Heydt because officials couldn’t get to their original assignments. We also didn’t even have an on-site replay assistant — those duties had to be handled from New York.

And OH MY GOD. The flags. 17 penalties for 152 yards. Compared to THREE for New Orleans.

Now listen. Were some of those penalties legitimate? Obviously. The Panthers absolutely need to clean up the stupid pre-snap stuff and stop giving teams free yards.

BUT SOME OF THOSE CALLS??? SOFT. AS. HELL.

Every five seconds there was another yellow flag on my screen. Horse collar. PI. Facemask. Holding. Existing too aggressively.

At one point it genuinely felt like Carolina could breathe near somebody and we were getting 15 yards.

And I do have to give Dave Canales credit for one thing… HE FINALLY GOT FIRED UP.

I actually saw him getting into it with the refs, defending his guys and showing some emotion on the sideline.

Maybe he read last week’s edition??? Because THAT is what I’ve been asking for.

You don’t have to lose your mind every game, but when your team is getting hit with call after call, sometimes I need to see you actually fight for them a little.

So yes, Dave. More of that.

And somehow we STILL WON. Which honestly makes it even funnier.

Chuba Hubbard basically dragged the offense down the field, Ryan Fitzgerald casually hit a 54-yarder as time expired, and after three straight losses we walked out of New Orleans… in first place.

Ugly? Extremely. But after the last few weeks? I will not be asking follow-up questions.

KEEP POUNDING 💙

🤯 Sports fun fact

The Panthers’ 17 penalties Sunday were a franchise record. But somehow that still isn’t close to the NFL record.

The Raiders were called for 23 penalties in ONE GAME in 2016. And somehow… they won that game too.

Apparently discipline is optional if you enjoy unnecessary stress.

💬 One thing to bring up

“Spain just lost for the first time in 43 matches.”

England beat them 3–2 in Madrid and officially ended one of the craziest unbeaten runs in international soccer.

Easy conversation starter. And now you sound like you’ve been following Nations League soccer all season. You’re welcome.

💭 Fi’s final thoughts

I’m just along for the ride.

This week really had everything. The FAA grounded the sports world. Ole Miss moved a college football game to Sunday and still lost. The Chargers finally won. Spain finally lost.

And the Panthers committed 17 penalties, kicked a walk-off field goal and somehow woke up Monday morning in first place.

I have officially stopped asking sports to make sense. I’m just along for the ride. And apparently so are the referees.

See you next week.

Now you know. 💚
