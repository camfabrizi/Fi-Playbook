For the people who don't watch sports… but still want to know what's going on.

🏆 What happened this week

Football is officially back.

I know, I know. It's only preseason. But after months without NFL football, I will absolutely be pretending this is the start of fall.

The preseason opened Thursday night with the Carolina Panthers beating the Arizona Cardinals 33–30 in the Hall of Fame Game. Carolina didn't exactly ease us back into football—quarterback Haynes King delivered the last-second touchdown.

Quick preseason explanation: these games don't count toward the regular-season record. Teams use them to test players, decide who earns a roster spot, and work out the kinks before the games actually start counting.

So no, we are not planning the Panthers' Super Bowl parade yet. But a win is a win. Keep Pounding.

🐐 From Carolina to Canton

Luke Kuechly gets his gold jacket.

If you were a Panthers fan in the 2010s, you definitely know Luke Kuechly. If you weren't, here's what you need to know: he was one of the best defensive players in football.

Kuechly spent his entire eight-year NFL career with Carolina. He was Defensive Rookie of the Year in his first season, Defensive Player of the Year in his second, and selected to seven Pro Bowls.

Now he's receiving one of football's biggest honors: a place in the Pro Football Hall of Fame in Canton, Ohio.

Eight seasons. One team. And now, forever a Hall of Famer. Very Carolina. Very deserved.

⚾ Baseball · 🎾 Tennis

The rest of the sports world is heating up, too.

Baseball's trade deadline has passed, which means teams have picked a lane: go all-in for a championship or start planning for next year. Now the playoff race gets serious, and every game matters a little more.

The U.S. Open starts later this month, so the tennis world is gearing up for one of the year's biggest tournaments. The Cincinnati Open is a perfect chance to learn a few names before everyone starts talking about tennis again.
